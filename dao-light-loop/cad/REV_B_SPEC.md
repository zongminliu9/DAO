# Rev B CAD specification

The checked-in STEP model is an engineering volume study. Rev B should become a manufacturing-aware assembly with separate bodies for:

1. decorative front shell;
2. replaceable optical window;
3. diffuser;
4. controlled aperture;
5. spectral-sensor island;
6. rigid-flex interconnect;
7. main electronics rigid section;
8. battery pocket and retention feature;
9. RF antenna keep-out / non-metal window;
10. rear cover;
11. clip / ear attachment mechanism;
12. charging contacts;
13. debug/test fixture interface.

## CAD parameters to expose

- face width / height / thickness;
- optical aperture diameter;
- sensor-to-diffuser distance;
- diffuser thickness;
- PCB/flex thickness;
- battery X/Y/Z;
- rear-cover thickness;
- clip offset and spring path;
- antenna keep-out dimensions;
- charge-contact spacing.

## Required drawings / renders

- assembled beauty view;
- exploded engineering view;
- front / side / rear orthographic;
- section through optical stack;
- section through battery/PCB;
- antenna keep-out visualization;
- mass estimate by material once materials are selected.
